# Motion Design

Motion should communicate hierarchy, continuity, or state—not merely show off effects.

- Use controlled, purposeful geometric/path-based motion.
- Hero: a line or arc inspired by the official logo may trace a route while the headline appears. Keep content and CTAs immediately accessible.
- Use modest section reveals; do not animate every element.
- Avoid scroll hijacking, long intro sequences, excessive glow, and continuous heavy effects on mobile.
- Founder and partner carousels should support touch and keyboard interaction. Avoid aggressive auto-scroll; pause movement on hover/focus if auto-motion is used.
- Animate only verified statistics.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; preserve all content and functionality when motion is reduced.
