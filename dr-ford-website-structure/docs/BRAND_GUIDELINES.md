# Brand Guidelines

## Brand role
Dr. Ford is an AI education school. Communicate curiosity, clarity, creative confidence, and a credible approach to learning AI.

## Desired feel
Intelligent, not intimidating; experimental, not chaotic; contemporary, not trend-dependent; distinctive, not a generic AI template.

## Logo metaphor
The circular maze logo suggests exploration, paths, decisions, and discovery. Use it as an abstract design system: subtle arcs, line paths, highlighted routes, or section transitions. Do not turn every section into a maze or redraw the official logo.

## Palette
- `#080A09` primary background
- `#101411` secondary background
- `#171B18` surfaces
- `#F5F7F5` primary text
- `#A8AEA9` secondary text
- `#B7FF00` accent

Use lime sparingly for primary actions and meaningful active states. No orange.

## Imagery and tone
Prefer approved real photography, course imagery, projects, and original geometric graphics. Avoid generic robots, glowing brains, and fabricated partner logos. Write clearly and specifically; avoid unsupported claims such as “the best” or “guaranteed results”.
